The savings circle nobody can run off with

Every week, abuelas all over Mexico save in a tanda: ten friends chip in and one takes the whole pot, in turns. Hundreds of millions of people save this way, as susu, chit funds, gye, hui, tontines, pasanaku or arisan. Its weak spot has always been trust: the organizer who runs off with the pot.

This 74-second short tells that story, and shows how a Bitcoin Cash covenant fixes it: the pot can only go to whoever's turn it is. English and Spanish captions. The animation and the marimba soundtrack were generated in code (source), with AI assistance.
